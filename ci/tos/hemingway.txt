Who we are and what this policy covers Boondoggle Studio, LLC, doing business as Hemingway Editor App (“Hemingway,” “we,” or “us”), provides writing tools. This policy covers our web app, including its free version and Editor Plus, our Microsoft Word add-in, the related website, and communications about those services. It covers the Word add-in whether installed through a supported marketplace or organization-managed channel; the installation channel does not change these protections. The legacy standalone desktop product remains outside this policy's scope and has its own applicable notice. Personal information means information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be linked to a person. Contact us at support@hemingwayapp.com for privacy requests. You may also contact legal@hemingwayapp.com. Our mailing address is PO Box 72, Durham, NC 27702, United States. For some activities, we decide why and how information is used. Privacy laws call us a “controller” for those activities. When we handle personal information in writing or other material on a business customer's instructions, we generally act as its “processor”—a company handling information on its behalf. Our Data Processing Addendum applies to covered processing under the applicable terms, including qualifying self-service business use. A separate signature is available on request. If an organization uses Hemingway to process your personal information, contact that organization with questions about its use of that information. Additional notices may explain a particular feature or collection. They supplement this policy. 2. Your writing and AI features Your working document normally stays in your browser or, when you use the Word add-in, wherever you save your Word files. Hemingway does not provide a cloud document library. During an Editor Plus trial or paid plan, some AI features work automatically as you write; others run when you choose them. These features send the necessary text to Hemingway and the AI providers identified in our Service Providers and Subprocessors. This may include your instructions, a selected passage, surrounding context, or the full document text needed for the feature. Our providers return suggestions and other results. We treat your writing, prompts, instructions, and AI output as confidential. We use them to provide the service, protect it against security threats and abuse, help with support requests, meet legal requirements, and handle permitted retention and deletion. We and our AI providers do not use this content to train, fine-tune, or improve AI models. We do not enable voluntary provider sharing for model improvement. We do not sell your content, use it for advertising, or collect your writing to improve the product generally. These commitments describe our practices from this policy's effective date, not our historical practices. People do not routinely read your AI requests for Hemingway. Limited human access may be needed for requested support, specific security or abuse investigations, or legal requirements. AI providers may also allow limited human review for security, abuse monitoring, or legal requirements. 3. Cookies, website measurement, and email We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember settings, and protect the service. We also measure website visits, understand how people use features, and track referrals. Our basic website measurement does not use analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. Product analytics and referral tracking are optional. Our email provider records whether messages were delivered or bounced, along with spam complaints and unsubscribe requests. We do not track email opens or clicks. Where the law requires permission for optional technologies, we ask before using them and let you change your choice. Use Privacy choices in the user menu to allow or reject optional uses, or choose separately whether to allow product analytics and affiliate attribution. We honor legally required opt-out preference signals. Global Privacy Control (GPC) disables both optional purposes. Older “Do Not Track” signals are different and do not themselves change our practices. You can also control cookies in your browser. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in or other features from working. We do not use advertising pixels or cross-site advertising trackers on the services covered by this policy. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, meaning advertising based on activity across different companies' services. 5. How long we keep information We keep information for the purposes described in this policy. Our Retention Schedule explains the periods and deletion rules for each type of record. Canceling your paid subscription normally leaves your free account available. It does not close your account or delete the information needed to provide that service. We delete eligible account records and saved instructions within 30 calendar days after a valid deletion instruction or the end of the relevant processing on a customer's behalf, unless an earlier deadline applies. The DPA and Retention Schedule explain which records this covers and how to request a copy before deletion. Some records remain after account closure. We retain business correspondence indefinitely for support, billing, and relationship history, subject to applicable privacy requirements and the DPA where it applies. This includes text or attachments you send us for support, but not automatic retention of writing entered into the editor. We also retain financial and referral records, email preferences, and limited records of contract acceptance and privacy requests for their permitted purposes. AI providers may retain requests for security, abuse prevention, or legal requirements. Analytics, error reports, email delivery records, and backups follow the separate rules in the Retention Schedule. We review whether personal information is still needed and delete or deidentify it when its permitted purpose ends, unless the law requires retention. Closing your Hemingway account does not delete documents stored in your browser, Word, or storage controlled by you or your organization. Signing out does not clear your browser's working document; clear it yourself when appropriate, especially on a shared device. 8. Security We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and service, including access restrictions and encryption for data in transit and stored service records. Access to protected content is limited to authorized people and providers who need it for a permitted purpose. No system can guarantee absolute security. This does not limit our contractual or legal security duties. Please secure your account, device, and browser profile. Avoid sending unnecessary confidential material in a support request.
